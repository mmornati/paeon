# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, criticise ideas and code rather than people, and do
not harass anyone. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of the
project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here is unacceptable, contact the maintainer privately through the contact
options on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mmornati). Reports are kept confidential.
The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may ban a contributor temporarily or permanently.
