# Design System: Paeon

## Overview

Paeon has two visual worlds. They are separate on purpose, and a surface belongs to exactly one.

| World | Where it lives | Mode | Source of truth |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Console** | `src/paeon/ui/` (templates, `static/console.css`) | Operate | `console.css` |
| **Site** | `website/` (landing page), `docs/` through `docs/stylesheets/extra.css` (MkDocs), `website/videos/` (`kit.js`, `player-paeon.css`) | Persuade for the landing page, Read for the docs | `website/css/site.css` |

In the frontmatter, a token with no prefix belongs to the console. A token that starts with
`site-` belongs to the site, and one that starts with `docs-` exists only in the documentation
theme. Nothing crosses: the console never takes a `site-` token, and the site never takes a
console token. The two worlds share one idea and nothing else: what a model said is violet.
The site's model violet is the same value as the console's dark-scheme one.

Every section below has a **Console** part and a **Site** part.

### Console

The console is an incident management app, in the family of the tools an operator already
knows: a sidebar, a list of incidents grouped by where they stand, status as coloured badges,
a detail page with a summary header and a timeline. It is clean and quiet: neutral surfaces,
one accent, colour kept for state.

It replaces the earlier "Strip Bay" console. Three things carry over, because they come from
the product and not from the old look:

- **The model's words look different from what code recorded.** They are violet, and the
  section or line also says so in words (a `Model` badge, a `model` or `tool` tag). A fact about
  a model call (tokens, cost, failure) is plain text.
- **Severity and state are always also a word.** The colour of a badge is never the only sign.
- **Nothing is loaded from another host**, and there is no inline style: the content policy
  refuses both. System fonts, one stylesheet, two small scripts, inline SVG icons.

### Site

**Creative North Star: "The Go / No-go Board"**

The site is a launch-day status board. Each station of Paeon reports in turn on a deep navy
ground, in chalk-white condensed capitals, and answers with a lit cell: green GO, amber HOLD,
red NO-GO. The last row holds on YOU, beside a striped guard over the one switch that acts.
The board is the argument of the page: a visitor sees where thinking stops and doing starts
because the poll stops there.

The page is dark only (`color-scheme: dark`), flat, ruled and square. It has no card grid, no
shadow and no soft gradient. What reads as a sentence is set in Barlow in sentence case; what
reads as a label, a station or an answer is set in Barlow Condensed capitals.

The documentation wears the same board at reading size: condensed capital headings, amber
links, square code blocks and tables, with a plain Barlow body. It also has a light scheme
(MkDocs Material's white page under the navy header), where amber darkens to stay readable.
The three videos are drawn on a canvas from the same palette, copied by value into `kit.js`.

**Key Characteristics:**

- A navy board in four tonal steps, with hairline rules between rows.
- Status is a lit square cell with its word inside: GO, HOLD, NO-GO.
- Barlow Condensed capitals for stations, headings and keys; Barlow for sentences; JetBrains
  Mono for commands and catalog names.
- Square corners everywhere, and no shadow.
- One guarded switch: lift the hazard-striped cover, then press.
- Every example is fictional and says so on the board (`Simulated`) and in the footer.

## Colors

### Console

Every colour is a token in `console.css`, written once as `light-dark(light, dark)`; the
frontmatter lists both values (`-dark` suffix). The scheme follows the system, unless the
operator used the switch in the sidebar (`data-theme` on `<html>`, kept in `localStorage`).

- **Neutrals**: `bg` (page), `surface` (cards, sidebar, inputs), `surface-2` (table heads, code,
  notes, neutral badges), `border` and `border-strong`, and three text levels (`text`, `text-2`
  for secondary lines, `text-3` for labels and times).
- **Accent** (`accent`, `accent-bg`): links, focus ring, the current page in the navigation, a
  pressed button, citation chips, meters and chart bars, an incident that just changed.
- **Model** (`model`, `model-bg`): what a model said, and the dot of the Investigation stage.
- **State**: `crit` (critical, firing, failed, the kill switch), `warn` (warning, draft,
  cancelled, a claim with no tool call behind it), `info` (info severity, waiting, running),
  `ok` (resolved, verified, a stage that is on). Each has a tinted `-bg` for its badge.

State colours appear on badges, on the 3px severity edge of an incident, and on a state value
inside an incident's boxes. They are not used for decoration.

### Site

A night board: four navy steps for ground, three cool whites for ink, and four lights. Each
light has one meaning. All values are custom properties at the top of `site.css`.

#### Primary

- **Hold Amber** (`site-amber`): the working light of the board. It is HOLD and it is you: the
  YOU row, the hold lamps, the countdown numerals and the clock. It is also everything you can
  follow or press: links, the solid Quickstart button, the focus ring (2px, offset 3px), the
  text selection, the guard's stripes. **Lit Amber** (`site-amber-lit`) is its hover on the
  solid button only.

#### Secondary

- **Go Green** (`site-go`): GO, and what has been cleared or verified. GO lamps, the word "Go"
  in the headline, the Go key, `T-0`, the five verified runs, the Auto level, a catalog action
  that exists. **Lit Green** (`site-go-lit`) is the hover of the solid Go key at the end of the
  page.
- **Abort Red** (`site-nogo`): NO-GO only. The No-go key and a declined answer.

#### Tertiary

- **Model Violet** (`site-model`): what a model said. The finding of a station whose voice is a
  model, and the solid `Model` voice tag.

#### Neutral

- **Board Navy** (`site-bg`): the page.
- **Deep Navy** (`site-deep`): the top strip, the videos band, the closing band, command
  blocks, the pictured phone, and the ink of any text that sits on a lit colour.
- **Panel Navy** (`site-panel`): a board, the dial, the monitor.
- **Raised Navy** (`site-panel-2`): the YOU row, and the pictured notification.
- **Rule Blue** (`site-line`): every hairline, and the border of an unlit lamp. At 35% it draws
  the hatching of the blanked catalog position.
- **Chalk** (`site-ink`): headings, station names, and the solid ground of a selected tab.
- **Soft Chalk** (`site-soft`): running text under a heading, findings on the board.
- **Faint Chalk** (`site-faint`): fine print, captions, unlit lamps and unselected tabs.
- **Terminal Ink** (`site-code-ink`): the text of a command block, on the site and in the docs.
- **Stage Black** (`site-stage`): the ground behind a video frame, nowhere else.

The documentation adds four values: `docs-ink` (body text in the dark scheme),
`docs-footer-deep` (the lower footer), and for the light scheme `docs-link-light` (links) and
`docs-accent-light` (hover and active), which are Hold Amber darkened for a white page.

#### Named Rules

**The Lamp Rule.** A status colour is a lit cell with its word in it. Green is GO, amber is
HOLD, red is NO-GO; the cell is filled, its text is Deep Navy, and the word is always there.
An unlit cell is a Rule Blue outline with Faint Chalk text. A cell at work (`Apply`, `Verify`)
is an amber outline with amber text.

**The Violet Is A Voice Rule.** Violet marks words a model produced, on the site as in the
console, and a `Model` tag stands beside them. It is never an accent.

**The Ink On Light Rule.** Text on amber, green, red, violet or Chalk is Deep Navy. Chalk text
never sits on a lit colour.

## Typography

### Console

System sans for everything read, system monospace for what is counted or cited: ids, times,
costs in tables, event kinds, catalog actions, payloads. Tabular numerals are on for the page.

- **Title** 1.5rem/700: the page heading. **Lead** 1.0625rem/500: the first statement of a card.
- **Body** 0.9375rem. **Small** 0.8125rem: secondary lines, tables, notes.
- **Label** 0.75rem/600: badges, box labels, table heads. Sentence case; uppercase is kept for
  the group headings of the board and the sub-headings inside a card.
- **Figure** 1.5rem/700: the number of a stat or of a pipeline tile.

### Site

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (with Arial Narrow, sans-serif), weights 500, 600, 700
**Body Font:** Barlow (with -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif), weights 400, 500, 600
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, Menlo, monospace), weights 400, 600

**Character:** A signboard face and its text companion. The condensed capitals are tall,
tracked and loud like lettering on a status board; Barlow underneath is plain and easy at
reading size. Tabular numerals are on for the page, and ligatures are off in code.

#### Hierarchy

- **Display** (`site-display`, 700, fluid 3rem to 5.75rem, line-height 0.94): the one headline
  of the first screen.
- **Headline** (`site-headline`, 700, fluid 2.25rem to 4rem, line-height 1): the heading of a
  band, and the closing question.
- **Rule** (`site-rule`, 700, fluid 1.625rem to 2.375rem): a flight rule, stated as a heading.
- **Answer** (`site-answer`, 700, 2.25rem): the level named by the dial, and the last Go key.
- **Count** (`site-count`, 700, 2rem): the `T-5` to `T-0` numerals of the countdown.
- **Title** (`site-title`, 600 or 700, 1.75rem): a station in the poll, a sub-heading under
  the dial, the board's clock.
- **Station** (`site-station`, 600, 1.5rem, tracking 0.1em): a row's name on a board, a
  countdown step, the Go and No-go keys, the wordmark (700, tracking 0.18em).
- **Key** (`site-key`, 700, 1.375rem): the dial's four keys, the guard's cover, a video title
  in the reel (600).
- **Button** (`site-button`, 700, 1.25rem, tracking 0.12em): the two buttons of the hero.
- **Lamp** (`site-lamp`, 700, 1.125rem, tracking 0.12em): the word in a lamp, a monitor tab.
- **Nav** (`site-nav`, 600, 1.0625rem): the top strip's links and a board's heading line;
  `site-nav-compact` (1rem) under 60rem.
- **Tag** (`site-tag`, 700, 0.9375rem, tracking 0.16em): voice tags and the Copy key;
  `site-tag-small` (0.8125rem) for the `Simulated` mark.
- **Lede** (`site-lede`, 400, 1.1875rem, line-height 1.55, at most 34rem wide): the paragraph
  under the headline.
- **Body** (`site-body`, 400, 1.0625rem, line-height 1.6): running text, at most 46rem wide.
- **Note** (`site-note`, 0.9375rem): a finding on a board row, fine print, the footer.
- **Caption** (`site-caption`, 0.875rem): a figure caption or a tip.
- **Code** (`site-code`, 0.875rem, line-height 1.55): commands and inline code;
  `site-code-name` (600, 1.0625rem) for an action's name in the catalog.

In the documentation the same three faces are used at MkDocs Material's own root size, so the
values are smaller in rem: `docs-display` (page title), `docs-headline` (section, under a 2px
rule), `docs-title`, `docs-label` (tabs, table heads, admonition titles) and `docs-body`
(line-height 1.65).

#### Named Rules

**The Capitals Rule.** Capitals belong to Barlow Condensed, and to things that are one line:
a heading, a station, a key, a tag. Anything read as a sentence is Barlow in sentence case.
The heading line of a board (the incident's name) stays condensed but in its own case.

**The Tracking Rule.** The smaller the capitals, the wider the tracking: 0.01em on the
display, 0.06em to 0.1em on titles and stations, 0.12em to 0.16em on lamps, keys and tags.

## Layout

### Console

Phone first. One column with a 1rem gutter; from 60rem a 15rem sidebar stands at the left and
the gutter widens to 2rem. Reading pages are capped at 60rem, the board at 76rem.

- **Shell**: on a phone, the brand and theme switch, then the navigation as a row that scrolls
  sideways, then the stages on one line. From 60rem, a sticky sidebar: brand, navigation with
  icons, and the stages at the bottom with their state. The stages always say what is on.
- **Board**: a filter box, the pipeline (one tile per stage with its count, a link to that
  group), then the groups. Each incident is one card: severity badge, id, title, flags, when,
  and one labelled box per stage. Boxes are one line each; reasons are on the incident page.
- **Incident**: breadcrumb, a summary header (badges, title, the same boxes with their reasons),
  sticky section tabs, then one card per section: Alerts, Triage, Investigation, Plan, Record.
- **Ledger and Actions**: a row of stat tiles, then cards. Tables sit flush inside their card
  and drop their token columns under 44rem, printing them as a second line.

### Site

Phone first, one column, capped at 96rem. The side gutter is fluid (`site-gutter`), and a band
has fluid space above and below (`site-band`) and a hairline on top.

- **Top strip**: sticky, 3.5rem tall, Deep Navy: the wordmark with its amber square, then the
  links. Under 60rem only Quickstart, Docs and GitHub remain.
- **First screen**: from 68rem, five columns of copy and seven of board, centred on each other.
  Below that it is one column in this order: headline, lede, board, buttons, fine print.
- **Band head**: from 60rem the heading takes five columns and its paragraph seven, aligned on
  their last line. Below, they stack.
- **Rows**: content is a list of rows. A board row is name, finding, lamp on one line from
  44rem (the name column is 10.5rem, 17rem in the catalog) and stacks below it. Stations, rules,
  countdown steps and hold points are rows on the page itself.
- **Videos**: the one full-width band, on Deep Navy, with its content capped at 89rem: the
  frame, and from 68rem a 21rem reel at its right.
- **Breakpoints**: 44rem (rows on one line, the dial's keys in one row of four), 52rem (dial
  text beside the phone, countdown numeral beside its step), 60rem (band head, station columns,
  full navigation), 68rem (the two-column first screen, the reel).

The documentation keeps MkDocs Material's layout: header, tabs, navigation, content, contents.

**The Ruled Row Rule.** A list starts under a 2px Chalk rule, and each row ends on a 1px Rule
Blue hairline. Rows are not boxed, not shaded in turn and not laid out as a grid of cards.

## Elevation & Depth

### Console

Flat. Cards are `surface` on `bg` with a 1px border and, in light only, a 1px shadow.

### Site

Flat, with no shadow at all. Depth is tonal: Deep Navy sits behind the page, Panel Navy is a
board on it, Raised Navy is the row that waits for you. A 1px hairline marks every edge. The
one thing that leaves the plane is the guard's cover, which hinges up from its top edge.

**The No Shadow Rule.** Nothing on the site casts a shadow or glows. A lamp is lit by its
fill, not by a halo.

## Shapes

### Console

Corners: 0.625rem for cards, 0.375rem for controls, full pills for badges and filter chips.

### Site

Square (`site-square`). Lamps, buttons, keys, boards, tabs, tags, command blocks, images and
the amber mark are all rectangles with hard corners. Borders are 1px for structure and 2px for
anything lit or pressable: a lamp, a button, a key, a voice tag. Two striped fills exist, both
hard-edged at 45 degrees: the amber and navy hazard bars of the guard's cover, and the fine
hatching over the catalog position that does not exist.

**The Square Rule.** No radius on the site, the docs or the video player. The one exception is
a picture of another device: the phone and its notification keep a phone's round corners
(`site-pictured-phone`, `site-pictured-notification`, `site-pictured-notification-key`), on
the page and in the videos. Those three values are for that picture only.

**The Hard Stripe Rule.** Stripes have hard stops and mean "guarded" or "blanked". A gradient
that fades from one colour to another is not used anywhere.

## Components

### Console

- **Badge**: a pill, 1.375rem tall, label type, tinted by state, with a dot when it is a state.
  Variants: severity, incident status, alert state, plan outcome, risk, `Model`, tag (outlined:
  flapping, maintenance), `updated` (solid accent, set by the live refresh).
- **Pipeline tile**: stage name with its dot, and the count as a figure. Empty is dimmed. "Your
  answer" is tinted with the accent when a plan is waiting.
- **Incident card**: 3px severity edge at the left, dimmed when resolved. The whole card is the
  link. A state value in a box is coloured and dotted by tone: active, bad, good, warn, mute.
- **Card** (`.block`): a head (title, and a badge or a fact at the right) over a body. Inside:
  lead, facts, a note (tinted, with a left rule: what was not done, what a thing cannot do),
  bordered lists (alerts, evidence, plan steps with numbered circles).
- **Record row**: time in monospace, a voice tag (`in` blue, `code` neutral, `model` and `tool`
  violet, `sent` green, `you`), the line, then its kind, cost and duration in monospace. A
  disclosure shows the payload. The row a citation points to is tinted with an accent edge.
- **Cite chip**: `c1`, `c2` in monospace on the accent tint, a link to the tool call. When no
  tool call stands behind a claim it is an outlined warning chip that says so in words.
- **Document** (`.md`): a postmortem rendered from its markdown on one card, prose capped at
  46rem: ruled section headings, tinted quotes, bordered tables, code on `surface-2`, action
  items as ticked or empty boxes. The source sits under it in a disclosure. A `mermaid` block
  is drawn by the bundled library in a bordered figure, in its neutral or dark theme to match
  the scheme; that page alone allows inline styles, because the library needs them.
- **Buttons and fields**: 2.5rem tall. One neutral button; pressed is solid accent; the kill
  switch, and removing a channel, are solid red. A form has one filled button (`.btn-primary`,
  solid accent): the one that saves it, or signs in. Nothing else is filled.
- **Form** (`.form`, channel settings and sign-in): one column of fields capped at 30rem. A
  field is a label, its control, then one line of help; a secret carries a small outlined tag
  with a lock (`secret`, or `kept` once stored) and is never printed back. What is wrong is
  said in red under its field and counted in a banner above the form. A choice with a
  consequence is a bordered row with its checkbox, tinted with the accent when ticked.
- **Pick list** (`.pick`): rows on one card, each a whole-row link: a name, one line on what
  it is, a chevron. Used to choose the service of a new channel.
- **Banner**: red for what failed, green (`.banner-ok`) for what was just saved, sent or
  removed. Always a sentence, placed under the page head.
- **Origin tag**: an outlined tag with a lock, `rules file`, beside a channel the console
  shows and cannot change.
- **Sign-in and setup** (`.door`): the two pages with no shell: a single card in the middle
  of the screen, with the mark, its fields and the filled button.
- **Getting started** (on the board, for an admin, until done): one card of numbered steps,
  the plan-step list reused. A done step turns its number green and carries a `done` badge;
  the first step left has the filled button, the others the neutral one.
- **Stat tile, meter, chart**: a label, a figure, a line under it. Meters and the bars of the
  spend chart are SVG whose sizes are attributes, in the accent; a spent budget turns red and
  says "reached".

**Motion.** On live refresh (every 10 seconds, paused while the tab is hidden or focus is on
the board) an incident that changed stage slides to its new place over 320ms, and is ringed and
tagged `updated` until pointed at or focused. Hover states ease over 150ms. Under reduced
motion there is no movement and no smooth scroll.

### Site

Controls are heavy and literal, like switches on a desk: tall, square, outlined in 2px, and
filled solid when lit or pressed.

#### Lamp

- **Shape:** a square cell, 2.125rem tall and at least 6.25rem wide, with a 2px border.
- **States:** unlit (Rule Blue outline, Faint Chalk word), GO, HOLD, NO-GO (filled, Deep Navy
  word), at work (amber outline and word). The fill changes over 180ms.
- **The waiting lamp:** the HOLD lamp of the YOU row blinks in two hard steps every 1.4s. It
  is the only thing on the page that repeats.

#### Board

- **Style:** Panel Navy with a 1px hairline around it. A head line (the incident, the
  `Simulated` tag, the amber clock), then rows, then a desk line that says what just happened.
- **Row:** station name, finding, lamp. The finding is Soft Chalk when code recorded it,
  violet when a model said it, amber when it is yours. A row not yet called is at 38% opacity.
- **The YOU row:** Raised Navy, and it holds the guard.
- **Variant, the switch bank:** the same rows for the action catalog: the name in monospace,
  wider lamps, and a last hatched row for the position that does not exist.

#### Guard and keys (signature)

- **Cover:** hazard stripes with an amber plate that carries `Lift the guard`. It lies over
  the two keys. Pressing it swings it up from its top edge (500ms) and enables the keys.
- **Keys:** Go and No-go, 3.5rem tall, outlined in their own colour, filled on hover and focus.
  After an answer both dim to 35% and the executor's rows run Apply, then Verify.
- **Closing keys:** at the end of the page the Go key is solid green and 4.25rem tall, and the
  No-go key is an outlined link to the threat model.

**The Two Step Rule.** The control that acts is under a guard: lift, then press. Nothing else
on the site takes two steps, and nothing that acts takes one.

#### Buttons

- **Shape:** square, 3.25rem tall, 2px border, condensed capitals.
- **Primary:** solid Hold Amber with Deep Navy text; Lit Amber on hover. One per screen.
- **Outlined:** Soft Chalk border and Chalk text; on hover it fills with Chalk.
- **Text link:** amber, underlined 1px (2px on hover). A link that leads out of a band ends in
  a drawn arrow (an SVG mask in the link's colour).

#### Voice tag

- **Style:** a small square label after a station's name. `Code` is outlined in Soft Chalk,
  `Model` is solid violet, `You` is solid amber. It names who speaks at that station.

#### Dial, tabs and reel

- **Dial:** four keys in a row (two by two under 44rem), 4rem tall, split by hairlines. The
  selected key is filled: Soft Chalk for Observe, amber for Suggest and Approve, green for Auto.
- **Monitor tabs and video reel:** the selected item is solid Chalk with Deep Navy text; the
  others are Faint Chalk and brighten on hover.

#### Navigation

- **Top strip:** condensed capitals in Soft Chalk; on hover the text turns Chalk and a 3px
  amber bar appears under it. The wordmark is preceded by a small amber square.

#### Command block

- **Style:** Deep Navy with a 1px hairline, monospace in Terminal Ink, scrolls sideways. A
  `Copy` key may stand at its right: Panel Navy, turning solid amber on hover.

#### Documentation

- **Headings:** condensed capitals; a section heading sits under a 2px rule in the text colour.
- **Blocks:** code, tables, admonitions and images are square with a 1px hairline. A warning
  has an amber border and a solid amber title with Deep Navy text.
- **Header and tabs:** navy, a hairline under each, the current tab in amber.

#### Videos

- **Canvas:** the same board drawn by `kit.js`: lamps, rows, the phone, the guard as a toggle.
- **Player:** square controls with a 2px border; what is on is solid amber with Deep Navy text.

**Motion.** The poll is called row by row: each row comes up from 38% opacity in turn, about
0.6s apart, and its lamp lights. After Go the executor steps through Apply and Verify. Fills
and colours change over 180ms. Under reduced motion nothing animates and the poll is shown
complete.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

**Console**

- **Do** mark every word a model produced: violet, with a Model badge or a model tag nearby.
- **Do** write severity and state as a word inside the badge.
- **Do** say what was not done in a note: a stage that is off, a failed call, a spent budget.
- **Do** define a new colour as one `light-dark()` token and check both schemes.
- **Do** keep targets at 2.5rem for buttons, fields and navigation on a phone.

**Site**

- **Do** put a status in a lamp, with its word: GO, HOLD, NO-GO, or the fact itself
  (`In catalog`, `Proposed`, `Blanked`).
- **Do** set text on a lit colour in Deep Navy.
- **Do** lay new content out as ruled rows under a 2px Chalk rule.
- **Do** keep violet for a model's words and put a `Model` tag beside them.
- **Do** mark a simulated incident as simulated on the board that shows it.
- **Do** keep sentences in Barlow, sentence case, at 46rem wide at most.

### Don't:

**Console**

- **Don't** load a font, script, image or style from another host, or write a `style` attribute.
- **Don't** use a state colour for decoration, or violet for something code recorded.
- **Don't** let an incident card on the board grow: reasons belong to the incident page.
- **Don't** add a control that approves or runs anything: the console shows, can stop, and
  with `ui.settings` on configures where it sends. It never configures what acts.
- **Don't** print a stored secret back into a page, or put a second filled button in a form.

**Site**

- **Don't** round a corner, except on the picture of the phone.
- **Don't** add a shadow, a glow or a gradient that fades. Hard stripes belong to the guard
  and to the blanked position.
- **Don't** use green, amber or red for anything but go, hold and no-go, and what you can
  press.
- **Don't** set a sentence in capitals, or Barlow Condensed in a paragraph.
- **Don't** bring a console token, a pill badge or a rounded card onto the site, or a `site-`
  token into the console.
- **Don't** put a second solid amber button on a screen.
